Interactive companions to my strong-lensing / dark-matter substructure work (project write-up and paper in preparation).

- **[Dark Matter Lab](/space/dark_matter_lab.html)** — a 3D cosmic web (512k particles, Zel'dovich ΛCDM) and a strong-lens sandbox: drag a perturber onto an Einstein ring and watch the arcs respond.
- **[Clump Atlas](/space/clump_atlas.html)** — where two substructure detectors (a parametric scan and a U-Net) point on 300 simulated lenses with hidden truth: correct finds vs. false alarms, in the image plane.

Both pages are self-contained HTML; nothing is tracked or sent anywhere.
