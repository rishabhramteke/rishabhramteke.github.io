# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Hiring managers and recruiters for ML scientist roles (primary).** They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or an application and want to judge, quickly, what Rishabh works on and how well: projects, write-ups, publications and the résumé.
2. **Friends and readers (secondary).** They come for the personal journal: travel stories, side quests and blogs.

## Product Purpose

Rishabh Ramteke's personal website. It presents him as someone aiming for ML scientist roles (computer vision, geospatial and LiDAR ML) and, after that, keeps a personal journal of travel, side quests and writing. Success: a hiring manager reaches the strongest work and the résumé within a minute, and a friend can still find the stories.

## Positioning

The site of one person: real projects with honest results (for example the embed2heights write-up, where the simple baseline won), interactive space demos, a curiosity map, and travel stories, rather than a template portfolio.

## Operating Context

- Jekyll site on GitHub Pages, repo `rishabhramteke/rishabhramteke.github.io`, branch `master`, built from the Forty theme with heavy custom layouts (`_layouts/home.html` has a custom loading screen and cursor).
- Sections and routes that must keep working: home `/`, About `/aboutme`, résumé `/resume` (PDF in `assets/files/`), projects (`_posts/projects`), blogs (`_posts/blogs`), publications (`_posts/publications`), Travel `/travel`, Side Quest `/side-quest`, Curiosity Map `/curiosity-map/`, space demos (`/space/…`), contact `/#contact`.
- No local Jekyll toolchain is installed at present.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content, permalinks and existing post front matter stay; the redesign changes presentation, not URLs.
- Location is Amsterdam, Netherlands. The site does not name his current employer; LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/rishabhramteke7699/) carries that (confirmed 13 Nov 2026).
- The About page copy is out of date (still names a former employer); update the location, drop the employer, link LinkedIn.
- Nothing is pushed or deployed without Rishabh's explicit approval; samples are shown first.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rishabh Ramteke.
- How he describes himself on the site: "Cartographer and ML scientist working on computer vision and maps" (13 Nov 2026). Show the coordinates (52°22′ N 4°54′ E) and the H3 resolution-7 cell 871969526ffffff.
- Colour direction given by Rishabh: wine red, with a passionate, elegant, wise feel (13 Nov 2026).
- Motion: Rishabh likes the current home page because objects float and drift and it feels dynamic, not a static site; keep that energy, but not flashy (13 Nov 2026). Keep a short intro moment and a custom cursor, restyled.
- Avoid: feeling corporate, or flashy.
- Map lettering: heights and contour values are fine, but no place or peak names on the map (13 Nov 2026).
- Existing quote in `_config.yml` description: "You only live once, but if you do it right, once is enough" (Mae West).

## Product Principles

1. Work first: the path from landing to strongest project and résumé is short and obvious.
2. Honest and specific: show real results and real artefacts, never filler claims.
3. One person's voice: the journal sections stay personal and warm, not corporate.
4. Nothing breaks: every existing URL and page keeps working through the redesign.
