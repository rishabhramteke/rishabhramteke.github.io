My planning notes for walking two pilgrimages in Japan in 2027. The page has every day of the itinerary, lodging, gear, budget and booking timeline.

- **Spring, 16 March to 5 April 2027:** four days on the Kumano Kodo Nakahechi to the three Grand Shrines, then temples 1 to 23 of Shikoku on foot, timed for the cherry blossom.
- **Autumn, late October to mid-November 2027:** temples 24 to 88 in the autumn leaves, walking the dense stretches and taking buses and trains across the empty ones.
- **Two certificates in one year:** the Dual Pilgrim certificate at Kumano Hongū Taisha, which needs the Camino de Santiago I have already walked, and *kechigan*, completing all 88, at Ōkubo-ji.

The spring walk sits inside a ten-week loop out of Amsterdam, through Kolkata, Tokyo, Seoul, Jeju and Taiwan, chasing the blossom front north.
