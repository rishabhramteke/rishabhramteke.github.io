In 2026 I walked the last five days of the Camino Francés, from Sarria to Santiago de Compostela: about 115 km on foot through the hills of Galicia.

Arriving on foot earned me the *compostela*, the pilgrim certificate from the cathedral in Santiago. That certificate is half of the Dual Pilgrim recognition I am going for on the Kumano Kodo in Japan in 2027; the [trip planning page](/shikoku-88-trip-planning) has the plan.
