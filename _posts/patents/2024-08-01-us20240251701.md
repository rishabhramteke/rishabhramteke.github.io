An autonomous mower that checks its own work. While it mows along a recorded route, a camera facing backwards photographs the grass it has just cut, and the controller evaluates the shape of that cut vegetation to judge the mowing height and the finish quality, so the mower can adjust its cutting height. A work management system can also do this evaluation on a remote controller.

The invention was filed as two US applications, both published on 1 August 2024. The second, a continuation-in-part, adds the method of evaluating the group of cut vegetation from the rear camera's images and a computer-readable medium carrying that method.

- [US 2024/0251701 A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240251701A1/en): the first application, US 18/161,897, filed 31 January 2023.
- [US 2024/0251704 A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240251704A1/en): the continuation-in-part, US 18/593,999, filed 4 March 2024 with the January 2023 priority date.

Inventors: Kenji Nishimiya, Nikunj Shah, Rishabh Gopichand Ramteke. Assignee: Honda Motor Co., Ltd. Both are also listed on my [Google Scholar profile]({{ site.scholar_url }}).
