An autonomous mower that checks its own work. While it mows along a recorded route, a camera facing backwards photographs the grass it has just cut, and the controller evaluates the shape of that cut vegetation to judge the mowing height and the finish quality. The application also covers a work management system in which a remote controller does this evaluation.
