Scope: the landing page of rishabhramteke.github.io. Mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience: hiring managers for ML scientist roles first; friends and readers second. Action: reach the strongest work and the résumé within a minute; find the journal below.
Constraints: wine red, passionate, elegant, wise (user); keep a short intro moment and a custom cursor, restyled (user); not corporate, not flashy (user); no employer named; Amsterdam; LinkedIn linked; every existing URL keeps working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one surveyed sheet. Work is plotted as points on a real terrain, the journal is a dashed route across it, and an index lists every entry by grid reference. It refuses the dark developer portfolio and the headline-plus-card-grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool survey paper ground (#EDEFEC), wine overprint for contours and symbols (#8A2A3D), deep wine for titles (#5B1426), wine-black ink (#231518). Ruled neatline with lettered and numbered grid ticks, a legend box, a title block. Landform labels in spaced caps of a grotesque; route and water labels in italic serif. No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners.

STORY: In one look the visitor reads who this is (ML scientist: vision, geospatial, LiDAR; Amsterdam), sees the plotted work, and opens a project or the résumé. Scrolling, they meet the gazetteer, then Sheet 2, the journal route, then the imprint with contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width sheet inside a neatline. Real contours from a public DEM in wine. Legend box top left: name at display size, a one-line role, symbol key, links. Five to seven project and demo points plotted with labels. Title block bottom right: Sheet 1 Work, Sheet 2 Journal, Résumé, LinkedIn, Contact. The primary actions (a project, Résumé) are live links on the sheet.

FORM: Survey sheet, position 1 on the ordered list (IMPECCABLE’S PICK, chosen by the user over the assigned Figure plates), seed:cc8dc0236d6cbda1. Signature interaction: the cursor is a survey crosshair with a live grid reference and elevation readout read from the DEM; hovering a plotted point lifts its survey note with the ground height under it and draws a dashed range ring. Motion grammar (user asked to keep the current page’s floating, dynamic feel): the sheet is printed in separate overprint layers (contours, index contours, plotted points) that drift apart with the pointer and rise at different rates on scroll; each plotted point floats on its own slow drift and is pulled a little toward the cursor; the intro draws the contours in once (about 1.2 s, content visible from the start). The journal keeps the travel globe, redrawn as a wine-ink graticule with the lived-in route. All of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
